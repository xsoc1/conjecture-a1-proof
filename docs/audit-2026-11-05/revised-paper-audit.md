On `W=K^3`, the proposed natural matrices satisfy `[E12,E23]=E13`. The dual action `D(a)=-a^T` is a Lie representation, with `[-E21,-E32]=-E31`, the correct image of the central generator. On tensor products, generators act diagonally and different factors commute, so the Heisenberg relations persist.

For `v=epsilon_1^(tensor p) tensor e_3^(tensor q)`, `X` can act nontrivially at most once on each of the `p` dual factors; it vanishes initially on every natural factor. Similarly `Y` acts at most once on each natural factor and vanishes initially on every dual factor. Thus the required vector annihilation hypotheses hold.

Each single-factor `T_i=X_i+Y_i` has cube zero, with `T_i^2 epsilon_1=epsilon_3` and `T_i^2 e_3=e_1`. As printed, `j=i` is allowed and gives `p=-a_ii=-2`; the written nilpotence powers are then undefined. The intended off-diagonal Serre statement is correct after this local restriction.

The passage from `e_j` to `E_j` at lines 623–625 is now sound: `sigma(E_i)=E_i` transports the zero relation with the same fixed exponent to each distinct orbit vector, and summing gives the desired orbit sum. The scalar factors for `F_i,F_j` do not change a zero bracket. No further application gap was found under the explicit intended orbit hypotheses and the parent's verified classification.

### R2. False necessity assertion survives in techdoc

`techdoc.tex:294` still claims `Z^k B_m in I iff 2k+m>=2p+2q+1`. This is false for `p=1,q=0,k=0,m=2`: `B_2=X^2/2+XY+Y^2/2` belongs termwise to `AX^2+AY`, yet degree 2 is below `N=3`. Replace the biconditional by the sufficient implication, and optionally reproduce the already-correct counterexample from revised `report.tex:778–780`.

This is the first substantively false mathematical assertion identified in the revised companion that is not merely a direction word or missing domain convention. It is unused by the accepted main proof.

### R3. Wrong root-normalizing substitution in techdoc

`techdoc.tex:261` says to substitute `t->t/c` to move the root `c` to 1. As an argument substitution this is incorrect: `f(t/c)` has a root at `c^2`, not generally at 1. State `g(t)=f(ct)` exactly as the main report now does. For example, `f(t)=t-2` over `Q` exposes the direction error immediately. The multiplicity lemma itself remains correct.

### R4. Invalid positive-characteristic example

`techdoc.tex:272` cites `t^p-t` as a failure of the fewnomial multiplicity bound in characteristic `p`. Its derivative is `-1`, so all roots are simple and it is not a counterexample. Use `t^ell-1=(t-1)^ell` in prime characteristic `ell`: it has two nonzero terms but multiplicity `ell` at the nonzero root 1.

### R5. Integral line parameters in the standalone companion

`techdoc.tex:212–216` permits any integer `delta`, although `M=(N-delta)/2` must be integral for the powers defining `P_b`. Specify `delta congruent N mod 2` and, when parametrizing nonempty degree-`N` lines, `|delta|<=N`; equivalently start from a degree-`N` monomial as the main paper does. The main paper already handles this correctly. The companion's claim to be self-contained makes the restriction worth stating explicitly.
